Diva VR for Apple Vision Pro (visionOS 2 or newer)
Team Diva: Eva, Carl, Vicky · DesComp Project 2 (DigiPhant) · https://github.com/EvaLuglitched/Diva (branch carl/diva-vr)

This build is signed for development by Carl's Apple developer team, so it installs only on Vision Pro headsets
registered to that team. Anyone else needs to be added to the team, or build it from the carl/diva-vr branch with
their own team (see Assets/DivaVR/README.md in the repository).

INSTALL (Mac with Xcode, headset paired and unlocked)
  Xcode > Window > Devices and Simulators > select the Vision Pro > drag "Diva VR.app" into "Installed Apps", or:
      xcrun devicectl device install app --device <headset id> "Diva VR.app"
The app is called "Diva VR" on the headset. It asks for hand tracking the first time: Allow.

CONTROLS (sitting in the cockpit on the elephant's back)
  Walk / run ........ lean forward (sit back to stop)
  Turn .............. tilt your head
  Spray water ....... look at a target and pinch (hold to keep spraying)
  Eat, drink, refill  thumbs-up for 0.4 s, or a hand to your mouth
  MEGA BUBBLE ....... raise both hands to eye level and hold
  Buttons ........... look at them and pinch; PAUSE is on the dashboard
