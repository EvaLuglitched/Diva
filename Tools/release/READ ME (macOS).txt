Diva for macOS (Apple silicon: M1, M2, M3, M4; macOS 12 or newer)
Team Diva: Eva, Carl, Vicky · DesComp Project 2 (DigiPhant) · https://github.com/EvaLuglitched/Diva

INSTALL
1. Drag Diva.app into Applications.
2. The app is not from the App Store, so the first time: right-click Diva.app > Open > Open.
   If macOS says the app is damaged or cannot be checked, open Terminal and run:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Diva.app
   then open it again.

CAMERA
As soon as Diva opens, macOS asks whether Diva may use the camera: click Allow. That is all.
The body tracking (MediaPipe) is built into the app and runs on this Mac; nothing is uploaded.
If you clicked Don't Allow: click the green ALLOW CAMERA button on the home page, switch Diva on in the
camera list, and come back: the game picks the camera up by itself.

CONTROLS (one player does everything; 3 PLAYERS splits them left to right)
  Walk / run ........ pump both arms up and down            keyboard W
  Turn .............. tilt your shoulders or head            A / D
  Aim the trunk ..... raise one arm                          U / I
  Spray water ....... push both open hands forward (hold)    Space
  Eat, drink, refill  thumbs-up for 0.4 s                    F
  MEGA BUBBLE ....... both hands above your head 0.8 s       E
  Menus ............. raise one arm = next skin, both hands up = start    arrows, Enter
  F1 setup panel · F2 frame rate · Esc skip

The keyboard works at any time, with or without the camera.
